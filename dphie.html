Delta Phi Epsilon - Pitt Chapter Website

Improving navigation, content organization, and the online experience for prospective members, parents, and campus partners.

ROLE

Website Designer & Manager

TIMELINE

2025

TOOLS

Wix, Figma, CSS

BACKGROUND

Delta Phi Epsilon's University of Pittsburgh chapter website serves as a central resource for prospective new members, parents, and campus partners. As the chapter's website designer and manager, I was responsible for creating and maintaining an online presence that reflected the chapter's values, activities, and member experience.

CHALLENGE

The website needed to communicate information clearly to multiple audiences while remaining easy to navigate and maintain. Frequent leadership transitions, recruitment cycles, and changing events also required a flexible structure that could accommodate regular content updates without compromising consistency.

SOLUTION

I used Figma to redesign page layouts and navigation, organized website content for different audiences, and maintained the site's visuals and information to reflect ongoing recruitment, events, and leadership changes.
