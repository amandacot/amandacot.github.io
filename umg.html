More Artist Storefronts at Universal Music Group

Building responsive, artist-branded e-commerce storefronts to create seamless shopping experiences for music fans.

For in-depth case studies from this internship, see The Last Dinner Party and Tye Tribbett.

ROLE

D2C Engineering Intern

COMPANY

Universal Music Group

TIMELINE

Summer 2024

TOOLS

Shopify, Liquid, HTML, CSS

BACKGROUND

During my internship at Universal Music Group (Fame House), I worked on direct-to-consumer Shopify storefronts for various music artists, supporting the development and customization of their online merchandise stores.

CHALLENGE

Each artist storefront needed to reflect a distinct visual identity while maintaining a consistent and responsive shopping experience.

A key challenge was migrating stores from Shopify 1.0 to 2.0 using UMG's customized theme while preserving each artist's existing content and branding. The storefronts also needed to accommodate changing merchandise campaigns.

SOLUTION

I transitioned artist storefronts to Shopify 2.0, incorporating their existing imagery and product information into updated templates while maintaining the look and feel of their original stores.

I gathered feedback on the updated storefronts to ensure the changes aligned with each artist's expectations.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I developed and customized Shopify Liquid templates and implemented navigation and layout changes to support artist-specific branding and responsive design.

I used developer blocks to work around theme limitations, including manually adding social media icons when the built-in customization options were insufficient.

I also provided feedback on UMG's customized Shopify theme, identifying inconsistencies in product alignment settings and recommending improvements to make storefront customization more intuitive.
