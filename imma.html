Irish Museum of Modern Art - Shopify Components & UX Work

Digital Systems & UX Intern – Summer 2025

IMMA’s online storefront and digital visitor tools needed to better reflect the museum’s evolving exhibitions while remaining accessible to a wide range of visitors, including those interacting with digital experiences in-gallery. Navigation, content hierarchy, and mobile usability all impacted how visitors discovered exhibitions and related merchandise.

Constraints

  • Shopify platform structure and theme limitations
  • Accessibility requirements for a public-facing museum
  • Diverse visitor needs, including mobile and in-gallery use

My Approach

  • Restructured homepage and collection layouts to better surface exhibitions and seasonal items
  • Optimized mobile layouts and contrast for accessibility
  • Conducted in-gallery evaluation of the Smartify app, observing how visitors interacted with digital interpretation tools
  • Documented usability issues and accessibility gaps to inform improvements

Impact

  • Made exhibition-related products easier to discover
  • Improved consistency between IMMA’s physical and digital visitor experience
  • Identified accessibility and usability opportunities in a real-world museum setting
  • Created cybersecurity awareness materials to help staff better understand digital safety practices
