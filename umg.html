Developing Artist Storefronts for Universal Music Group

Building responsive, artist-branded e-commerce storefronts to create seamless shopping experiences for music fans.

ROLE

D2C Engineering Intern

TIMELINE

Summer 2024

TOOLS

Shopify, Liquid, HTML/CSS

BACKGROUND

During my internship at Universal Music Group (Fame House), I worked on direct-to-consumer Shopify storefronts for various music artists, supporting the development and customization of their online merchandise stores.

CHALLENGE

Each artist storefront needed to reflect a distinct visual identity while maintaining a consistent and responsive shopping experience. Development also had to accommodate existing Shopify themes, platform migrations, and changing merchandise campaigns.

SOLUTION

I customized Shopify themes and storefront components to support artist-specific branding, improve layout consistency, and create responsive shopping experiences across devices.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I developed and customized Shopify Liquid templates, modified storefront components, and adjusted navigation and page layouts to reflect each artist's branding and create responsive experiences across different screen sizes.
