Delta Phi Epsilon – Pitt Chapter Website

Chapter Website Designer & Manager – University of Pittsburgh

I design and manage Delta Phi Epsilon’s chapter website as a central communication hub for prospective new members, parents, and campus partners. The site needs to clearly represent our values and member experience while staying easy to navigate and adaptable to changing leadership, events, and recruitment cycles.

Constraints

  • Frequent leadership turnover requiring easy content updates
  • Need to serve multiple audiences with different goals

My Approach

  • Structured the site’s information architecture to serve different audiences, including potential new members and parents
  • Designed page layouts and navigation to make key information easy to find
  • Maintained content, visuals, and layout each semester to reflect leadership and event changes

Impact

  • Created a consistent and accessible online presence for the chapter
  • Made recruitment and information easier for prospective members to understand
  • Ensured the site remained current and usable across leadership transitions
